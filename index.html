Your weekly cycle, step by step

Each sprint follows the same sequence—brief and angles, then references, structure, scripts, and publish-ready deliverables—keeping your team aligned week after week.

1

Brief & positioning

From product inputs, surface prioritized angles, hooks, and a living brief the whole team can share before budget goes live.

2

Category references

Gather high-performing short-form examples in your vertical so new concepts start from proof, not blank canvases.

3

Creative anatomy

Pull hooks, rhythm, proof moments, and CTAs from references into a blueprint writers and editors can follow.

4

Script variations

Draft several script paths from one brief so each render ties to a defined angle-and-hook combination.

5

Published-ready ads

Render short scenes, assemble with templates, add captions and aspect ratios, then export labeled files for TikTok and Instagram.

What you get every run

Campaign brief

Guided intake becomes an editable brief your team owns—built for channel operators, not prompt tinkerers.

Angle–hook grid

Map angles and hook options upfront so you test combinations systematically—not one-off lucky shots.

Scripts & shot plans

Lock narrative flow (hook → proof → CTA) and turn copy into scene-by-scene directions suited to short-form.

Batch queue

Queue a test batch by angle and hook, track status, and retry or fall back when a render fails.

Assembly & export

Template-led edits: stitch 5–15s scenes into final cuts with captions, thumbnails, and multi-format output.

Insights loop (roadmap)

Connect results to test variables so the next cycle doubles down on winners—not unfocused volume.

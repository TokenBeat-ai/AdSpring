Evidence tags& optional VOC

Every sell point carries an evidence tag (page, VOC, competitor, or your notes). If VOC is missing, that section is omitted and data sufficiency is labeled.

Sell Point Report Lite

Matches the skill spec and Lite templates: short sections, channel-agnostic, optimized for TikTok / Reels / Shorts filming.

1 · Product facts & top jobs

Facts-only product lines from the page or your paste, plus three motivation sentences and a deal-breaker.

2 · VOC map

If you supply quotes: praise/complaint themes and a short language bank. Omitted when there’s no VOC.

3 · Top 3 filmable sell points

Promise, proof on camera, risk — each with Evidence: page | VOC | competitor | user_notes.

4 · Hook selection

Primary, secondary, and do-not-use templates with short rationale.

5 · Script pack

Three default scripts (hook 0–3s, on-screen text, evidence shots, CTA). Ask if you want 6 or 9 variants.

6 · Compliance guardrails

Don’t-say examples, safer alternatives, and quote rules so claims stay defensible.

Conversation flow (skill rules)

Progressive inputs — we never block on missing data. Best-effort URL extraction; if reviews won’t load, paste three verbatim quotes and we continue.

1

Send

Minimum: one product URL or a 1–3 line card. Strongly recommended: 2 complaints + 1 praise verbatim, a VOC theme table, or competitor links.

2

Echo

We list what we received (product / VOC / competitor). Optional: platform + style, claims to avoid.

3

Generate

Reply generate (same language as your input). One-shot Sell Point Report Lite, kept short.

4

Enhance

After the report, new VOC or competitor notes produce a replacement enhanced final report.

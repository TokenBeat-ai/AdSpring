What the clone workflow produces

Pick a reference, map its structure, re-skin with your product, then export a test-ready cut that keeps the original hook intact.

1 · Reference ingest

Import a viral TikTok or Reels ad—from Ad Spy or your own swipe file—as the clone template.

2 · Structure mapping

Lock hook timing, segment order, and CTA placement from the reference before any generation runs.

3 · Product re-skin

Swap in your product shots, brand kit, and offer copy while the pacing stays true to the original.

4 · Hook preservation

Keep the opening line and visual beat that made the reference work—adapted to your category and claims.

5 · Polish & captions

Apply captions, supers, and a balanced audio pass tuned for muted mobile feeds.

6 · Export to test

Ship vertical and square masters ready to upload—named and tagged for your next creative test.
